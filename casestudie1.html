Overview

SVEN is a digital application developed alongside a portable strength-testing system. The app connects to the hardware via Bluetooth, receives measurement data in real time and helps users view, save and follow test results over time. The project was a collaboration between interaction designers, web developers, a sports researcher and the IoT company Jemac. Our UX challenge was to turn complex strength data into a clear and practical experience for coaches, fitness professionals and researchers

Problem

Strength testing systems can be complex and expensive, while the measurement data itself can be difficult to interpret quickly in practice. SVEN needed an interface that could connect the testing process, live measurements and stored results in one clear experience.

How might we make strength testing and performance data easier to manage, understand and follow over time?

Goals & Design Context

Goals

Make strength tests easier to perform and follow up

  • Present results and progress more clearly
  • Support both individual and group testing
  • Increase user confidence

Technical context

Offline-first

  • Bluetooth connection to hardware
  • Real-time measurement data
  • Local data storage

Design Focus

Clear navigation

  • Understandable data visualisation
  • Simple testing flow
  • Accessible to users with different levels of experience
